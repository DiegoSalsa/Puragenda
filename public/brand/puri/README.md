# Puri mascot

The source of truth is `src/components/brand/puri-mascot.tsx`. Export the SVG files with `npx tsx scripts/export-puri-mascot.tsx` after editing the component.

- `puri-avatar.svg`: tight crop for small icons.
- `puri-default.svg`, `puri-greeting.svg`, `puri-thinking.svg`, `puri-attention.svg`, `puri-success.svg`: full silhouette for UI and brand pieces. `puri-wave.svg` remains an alias of greeting.

Use the avatar at 24–48 px and the full silhouette at larger sizes. Keep the lavender faceplate, black outline, purple limbs, yellow antenna, and same body shape in every state.
